## 1. Stage Partners

**To:** their general or licensing address from yourstagepartners.com
**Subject:** Permission to list your monologues on ActorRise, with credit

Hi,

I'm Canberk. I'm an actor in Berkeley and I built a site called ActorRise that
helps actors find audition monologues. I'm writing to ask permission for
something specific, and I want to be upfront that I have not done it yet and
won't without a yes from you.

I'd like to list the monologues from your free monologues page on ActorRise,
each one credited to the play, the playwright and Stage Partners, each one
linking back to you.

Here's why I'm asking you first. My library is almost entirely public domain
right now, which means an actor searching for something contemporary gets
Sophocles and Ibsen. Over the last six months, 71 actors on the site searched
for a contemporary piece. I had nothing written after 1929 to give them. That
gap is not something I can scrape my way out of, because every contemporary play
is in copyright, which is exactly why this is a permission question and not a
technical one.

What I'd commit to:

1. Credit on every piece: title, playwright, Stage Partners. Same credit you
   already ask actors for.
2. A link to your page for the full play, on every monologue.
3. A cap on length, so it stays an excerpt and never a script.
4. Anything down on request, same day, no argument.
5. No scraping until you say yes, and nothing beyond the titles you name.

I already enforce this in code rather than by promise. My ingest refuses to
store text unless a rights basis is recorded for it, and the site refuses to
display text that fails the same check. If it helps, I'm happy to show you
exactly what a listing would look like before anything goes live.

What I'd love back: an actor who finds a piece of yours on my site and clicks
through to buy the script. That's the whole point for me. I'm one person
building this, so I'd rather start small and honest with a publisher who already
gives these away than go quiet and hope nobody notices.

If you're open to it, tell me which titles you'd want included and I'll work to
that list. If there's a form or a rights person I should go through instead,
point me there and I'll do it properly.

Thanks for reading this far.

Canberk
Founder and actor, ActorRise
actorrise.com
canberk@actorrise.com
